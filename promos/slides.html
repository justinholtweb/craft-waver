The thing most tools miss

Tax inside
the price

Commerce keeps included tax inside the item subtotal, where it doesn’t move the order total.

  • Book that subtotal straight to sales and you record tax as revenue
  • Waver takes it back out and books the liability
  • The order total is unchanged either way — which is why it goes unnoticed

Before anything is sent

It balances,
or it doesn’t go

Wave rejects an entry whose lines don’t balance — and accepts one that balances for the wrong reason.

  • Sub-cent rounding goes to a rounding account
  • Anything larger isn’t rounding, and is refused with the drift
  • Preview any order and see the exact rows first

Idempotency

Never posts
the same money twice

Wave’s API can’t read a money transaction back — so Waver never guesses.

  • The id comes from the order, never from a random UUID
  • A unique index makes a duplicate impossible, not unlikely

Refusals, not surprises

Would rather
do nothing

Every refusal names what is wrong and what to change. None of them is a stack trace.

  • Checked before the call, not reported after it
  • Only the accounts this order actually needs
●Only $60.00 of $178.45 has been capturedA money transaction says cash arrived. Recorded as skipped, with the shortfall.
●No Wave account is mapped for Sales taxNamed per account — an order without a discount is never nagged about a discounts account.
●This business is on Wave’s classic accountingMoney transactions don’t work there. Said on the settings screen, not on your first sale.
●$9.95 of shipping, which an invoice can’t representWave has no order-level charge. Told to you, rather than dropped from the total.
